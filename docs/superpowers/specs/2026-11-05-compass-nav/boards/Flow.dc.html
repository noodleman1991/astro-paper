click a slice and its section pours out of it, like the sketch. on a desktop the compass stays where it is and the slice itself grows: its edges carry straight out and end in one smooth front, with paper around it. the tiles inside fit into each other with even channels between. the titles share one circle, like a seal: blog and services over the top, portfolio under the bottom, no boxes. only on a phone does the compass step aside, because there is no room otherwise.
