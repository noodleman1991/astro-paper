03 · shapes nothing perfectly round, nothing perfectly straight — except the stickers

project one

tiles in a spill — each section pours out of its slice and its items are tiles that fit into each other, with even channels between. every tile has its own corner radii and seams that curve or tab into the next one; one dotted tile per spill is the lead. seams re-roll per visit.

torn edges — the outline of every spill, kept to a slight wobble. the same turbulence filter the clouds already use, so the edge looks cut, not drawn.

04 · motion slow, organic, always skippable (prefers-reduced-motion = plain fades only)

breathe · 7s — the compass inhales and exhales. spiro.

grow · 1.1s — the slice grows out of its own edge and settles; the tiles show next, then two small sparkles. hover grows it a third of the way. the portfolio dial turns one step at a time, .85s each.

hover me too

tilt · 350ms — cards rotate −1.6° and lift. never bounce.

05 · rules of thumb

  • one spot ink per section, never mixed
  • ink lines are 2px and wobble a little
  • lowercase for labels and headings, sentence case for real copy
  • curves for surfaces, sharp corners for small meta
  • hard offset shadows, never blurred ones
  • motion is slow and settles; nothing bounces
  • on a desktop the compass stays put; only on a phone does it step aside
  • each section pours out of its own slice, with paper left around it
  • a spill is its slice grown bigger: edges carried straight out, one smooth front, no blobs, no drips
  • titles share one circle, like a seal: two over the top, one under the bottom; never in boxes
  • more than fits? turn the dial on a desktop (its neighbours peek in at the edges); on a phone the projects stream through a still envelope; no "more" buttons
  • few tiles, lots of air: a title and one line each; the rest lives in the window
